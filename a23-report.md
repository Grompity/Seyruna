  the road never fetched — while the shown word named only the read

- **Evidence provenance LOST between stages — NO, at the artifact.** The
  row-level words (`kind`, `evidence`, `note`, `publisher`, `url`) rode
  the eval record whole (this audit located every claim from the record
  alone); the turn-level words rode the trace. Two real, SMALL losses
