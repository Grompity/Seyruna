## The three levels, and the verdict table (bytes-only, exhaustive)
- **Level A (retrieval diversity):** the read returned a row WITH BYTES
  (`text` non-empty at a `status`) AND its origin publisher stands
  DISTINCT from the primary's AND is not the house. A distinct HOSTNAME
  is never a distinct VOICE (A7: the publisher-word law).
- **Level B (evidential independence):** reads at A7's ceiling ONLY -
  the merged two-text word (`independence` at `none|candidate|unknown`,
  the conflict ladder at `insufficient|unknown|bytes-share|
  bytes-clash-possible`); lineage UNKNOWN unless the bytes themselves
  speak it; a wikipedia-family or syndicated second NEVER graduates to
  independent by hostname alone.
- **Level C (claim corroboration):** the PRIMARY's cite rides (W1's
  bytes, cited verbatim) beside the second row's `claim` word; the
  machine-class falls from the ladder (SHARE->SUPPORT; CLASH->a
  CONTRADICTION candidate; INSUFFICIENT/UNKNOWN->FAILS-TO-ADDRESS AT THE
  MACHINERY - the human-read cite rides BESIDE it, never instead).
- **The verdict (declared order):** the read lamed at an INFRA word
  (timeout/socket-class) -> **BLOCKED** (the ONE lawful retry, A35's
  law); lamed at a RECORDED word (a status-code, `wrong-kind`,
  `too-big`, `blocked:` guard) -> **MONOCULTURE-PERSISTS at the lam-
  word** (a research fact about THIS address - A10: a lane-word is a
  record, never a fallback); bytes came AND the publisher repeats
  (house OR primary) -> **MONOCULTURE-PERSISTS**; bytes came, the
  publisher DISTINCT, the ladder says `bytes-share` ->
  **CORROBORATED**; bytes came, the publisher distinct, anything else
  -> **DIVERSITY-ONLY**.
