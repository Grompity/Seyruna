# shelf_proposal.md — the First Shelf expansion: schema, record types,
# coverage plan. Proposed BEFORE mass-populating, per the sign-off letter.
# The governing principle, in the owner's words: do not make Ascended know
# everything — make it TRUSTWORTHY about what it knows, what it does not
# know, and WHY it is saying what it is saying.

## S1 — The schema needs ONE new field, and the ten prove it
The founder's ten already carry, per record: claim, work, who, date,
provenance ("primary" | "ours-synthesis"), level (the KS-0.1 enum,
DOCUMENTED included), edges, counterexample, evidence, locus, note.
That machinery alone answers SEVEN of the letter's ten demands:
multi-tradition without flattening (work/who), primary provenance
(provenance/locus), levels preserved (level), disagreements and
non-decisions (counterexample + edges), counterexample where
convergence is claimed (the field is POPULATED on all ten — the
founder's own discipline), empirical separated from testimony
(level + the Goyal record as the empirical lane). The gap is the
LAST distinction on his list — practical teachings separated from
metaphysical assertions — which is a CONTENT-TYPE, orthogonal to an
epistemic LEVEL. Proposal: one optional field, "kind":
  TEACHING      a practice instruction ("act, but not for the fruit")
  METAPHYSIC    a claim about what IS ("neti, neti")
  PSYCHOLOGY    a claim about how minds work ("what troubles you is
                your judgment" — the text's own, not the meme's)
  METHODOLOGY   how to weigh (the Kalama examination)
  WITNESS-STATE a reported state or episode, not an argument (the
                Butterfly dream)
  NON-DECISION  what a text explicitly DECLINES to settle
Rules of the field, so it cannot smuggle law: kind never substitutes
for level (a METAPHYSIC claim can be DOCUMENTED; a TEACHING can be
EMPIRICAL); the field is OPTIONAL (absence = the machine stays silent
where the data is silent — the same discipline as the levels); and it
is data, so the mutation doctrine holds it: flip a kind in the data,
behavior may not move until the field is EARNED by a check.

## S2 — EDGES RIDE: the expansion's primary act is not text
The shelf header already planned this in the founder's own words:
"Edges ride as data when convergence is tested (Question 2 will demand
some)." For card 2 the EDGE IS THE ANSWER: the Dhammapada, the
Upanishad, and the Gita can all surface for one seeker-question, and
whether they CONVERGE is not a fact the mouth may mint — it is a
recorded edge (CONVERGES-WITH / CONTRADICTS / DOES-NOT-DECIDE, the
fixtures' own vocabulary) or a recorded SILENCE. A NON-DECISION record
is the shelf's way of knowing that a tradition DECLINED a question —
the system's humility, made durable in data rather than style.

## S3 — Coverage plan: roughly ten new records, every one aimed
C1 (intuition vs the story): the Gita's strands teaching (sattva/rajas
— cool-and-boring vs hot-and-urgent as TRADITION'S datum, not the
mouth's psychology) + Marcus on appearances and assent. (2 new)
C2 (convergence): the atta/anatta pair — a Samyutta-line NON-DECISION
(the self-question refused outright) + an EDGE SET across records
2/5/6/1 marking exactly where the "same ridge" reading breaks, + one
INTERPRETIVE COUNTEREXAMPLE ("the Buddha's non-self is not the
Upanishad's Brahman"). (1-2 new + the edges)
C3 (identity without status): the Gita's Janaka passages (TEACHING:
"by action Janaka attained to perfection… the wise act, lest the
ignorant follow"); Marcus's Enchiridion 40 (TEACHING: "being good,
don't want to be called good"); ONE modern-spiritual-culture record
(ours-synthesis, INFERENTIAL — a description, not a datum). (3 new)
C4 (surrender vs responsibility): the Gita 3/18 duty-line (TEACHING:
the action the hour's role requires is not renounced by non-forcing);
optional Zhuangzi Cook Ding (the blade in the gap — yielding AS skill;
the WITNESS-STATE-vs-METAPHYSIC decision on it gets SHOWN at
population, not assumed). (1-2 new)
C5 (consciousness): the Samyutta NON-DECISION does double duty (the
unanswered questions — one record, two cards; the shelf stays small ON
PURPOSE); optional modern record: "no agreed definition of
consciousness; the 'hard problem' is a philosophical proposal, not an
observation" (INFERENTIAL — the anti-meme record). (1-2 new)
Total: ~9-12 new, the shelf lands ~19-22 — a targeted doubling, not a
library. Every new record carries work/locus/provenance/level/kind/
counterexample and at least ONE EDGE or a named silence.

## S4 — The gate before the funnel: the PROJECTION test
The expansion is accepted in two steps, and the first never wakes the
funnel: (1) PROJECTION — offline, retrieval alone, the five cards'
question+FACTS text: each card must surface >= 2 records AND the aimed
kinds must be among them (the counterexample and the NON-DECISION
included, when the card's lesson is about them), AND no stranger
record may surface on a counting word (the Marcus-artifact class).
The two named bounds (stem-equality, content-blindness) were deferred
UNTIL they bite a real card — here is where they first get the chance
to ripen the graduation question, ON EVIDENCE. (2) The live pass: the
five and the eight re-speak; the slice-visible protocol stands
(visible channel first, verdicts blind, trace after). And a card MAY
STILL GO UNKNOWN at a card where it is true — UNKNOWN is permitted to
win.
## S5 — What this phase is not
No Gate, no agents, no Soul Log, no posting, no personality engineering,
no UI polish. No record added merely to look capable: the test of the
expansion is that the five become MEANINGFULLY ANSWERABLE — and that
verdict belongs to the projection, not to the builder's optimism. The
Constitution is unmoved; if the larger shelf shows a genuine conflict
with the law, the conflict gets NAMED and the founder decides — the
only road by which a Draft 5 will ever be born.
