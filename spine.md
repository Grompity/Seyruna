## 1 — What the last round actually proved (and did not)

PROVED, inside the tested frame (one model family, single-shot protocol,
37 authored + 4 held-out surfaces):
- Injected law **changes behavior in one and the same mind** — it
  made the mouth shorter on 23/37 pairs, and the fourteen longers were
  nearly all distinctions bought, not ornament bought.
- The law's purchase is specific: it forbids **minted rank** (the
  naive mouth's characteristic error was warm — it scored, it lectured,
  it concluded). Hence the founder's maxim, adopted as the round's
  epitaph: *"The Constitution did not make Ascended nicer. It made
  Ascended less entitled."*
- The law's clauses can be **self-cited in behavior** (the mouth
  declined its own ritual twice, with reasons, and priced its
  ground-question before asking it) — inhabited, not obeyed.
- The **failure taxonomy works in practice**: the round found law-level
  zero, machinery several, prompt-style several, test-design two
  (filed against our own cards) — and the CONTROL marks (≈8 cards the
  naive mouth tied) prevent the round from self-congratulating.
- Pre-registration works: a frozen semantic prior met behavior, and
  the disagreement came out richer than either document alone.
NOT proved (record, so nobody inflates): generalization beyond this
model family; multi-turn lawfulness; the Gate, the Map, or any office —
all tested as *prose*, none as *software*. And the deep find, filed as
a design constraint: **a one-sentence answer at a live conjunction fails
by omission** — so the product cannot be a single mouthful; conformance
needs a *place where the law can run after the mouth has already
spoken*.

## 4 — The Spine (flow, with the two changes that matter)

USER → INTENT → RETRIEVE → ANALYZE (source+level) → SYNTHESIZE (typed
draft) → **CHECK (rule-pack)** → SPEAK (voice) → **AUDIT (final)** →
RESPONSE (+ trace)

- **INTENT** — parse {question, domain, mode: knowledge | interpretation
  | practice, urgency}. A cheap model call; the frame is code. Purpose:
  set permissions *before* any generation — Access before Application
  is a *routing* fact, not a virtue.
- **RETRIEVE** — claim-graph lookup over the seed shelf. Retrieval
  *infra*, not a model. MVP: grep + citation resolution; vectors only
  when the shelf outgrows a grep.
- **ANALYZE** — mostly CODE: every retrieved record already carries
  its epistemic level, provenance, disagreement edges (the schema does
  this work, so the model does not *re-decide* levels at runtime).
- **SYNTHESIZE** — the model, but constrained to emit a **typed draft**:
  claims tagged CLAIM / RECOMMEND / PERMIT / DO, with citations and the
  named uncertainty — not prose yet.
- **CHECK (the rule-pack)** — deterministic software over the typed
  draft + the facts-frame: conjunction counts (machine, never the
  mouth — the round proved the mouth miscounts), first-person-ban
  (a RECOMMEND without yoked conditions fails), no-pitch, no-minted-rank
  (unassented JUDGMENT ⇒ downgraded to INFERENCE), disclosure
  completeness, precedence (facts do the ordering).
- **SPEAK** — the model, with the **voice brief only** (no machinery in
  the mouth); the register law's anti-diction clause lives here, which
  is *where the leak is mended* (§5).
- **AUDIT** — cheap post-pass over the SPOKEN text: promotion check
  (did beauty smugg a level the typed draft did not carry — the IMPLY
  verb's whole point), no-pitch, stamp telemetry (the K-battery run as
  a counter). Failure here triggers re-speak or *plain* — never
  re-reasoning from the top.
- **THE TRACE** — every response carries what fired: verbs, levels,
  checks. The trace is the machine's visible constitution; it is also
  what lets the golden set grade stages, not only mouths.

The friend's two interrogations, answered in advance: the law runs at
**multiple points** (scope/UNKNOWN gate before retrieval; the
rule-pack before speaking; the audit after) — and the **five verbs are
the spine's type system**: the interface between law, machinery, and
mouth. IMPLY is special: it is not emitted, it is *detected*, at the
audit, by design.

## 5 — The Constitution as implementation: answer C (hybrid), with
precision. The law compiles into THREE artifacts, and the giant
single-prompt (option A) is rejected for cause the round showed —
starved budgets, the diction leak, and the mouth treating law as topic:
1. **The rule-pack** (machine-readable): each operative article →
   a deterministic check over typed data. This is law as *gate*,
   binding the spine, invisible to the seeker.
2. **The voice brief** (a distilled mouthful): a few paragraphs of the
   law's *disposition* — chronicler-not-guru, lamp at arm's length, no
   pitch, no minted rank, the ranked vocabulary banned from the mouth.
   This is where the diction fix lands, and the answer to (b): the
   law's nouns stay in the rule-pack and out of the voice brief until
   the offices they name are installed.
3. **The full text** (reference): loaded only where citation is wanted
   (tests, the adjudication table) — never as a mouthful.
The round's own evidence decides it: the injected law *is* enough to
make one mind behave; but its cost, its leaks, and its self-citations
prove it is behaving *with a topic*, where the spine needs law as a
**ground**. Machinery, exactly as the round demanded, can then be
touched without touching law — the diction fix will be a voice-brief
edit, no draft, no vote.

## 6 — Knowledge architecture (minimum schema, claim-first)
The unit is the **claim-record**, not the document:
{claim, attribution{work, author/tradition, date, edition-locator},
provenance-chain{primary | attributed-to | later-interpreter |
ours-synthesis}, epistemic-level{EMPIRICAL | PHILOSOPHICAL |
INTERPRETIVE | INFERENCE + UNKNOWN}, disagreement-edges (typed, in
BOTH directions — *contradicts* vs *does-not-decide*),
counter-example slot, supports/against links, evidence-status
(**"none FOR" ≠ "none AGAINST" — the B3 lesson, a first-class field**),
citation-locator.
The two anti-flattening invariants, storable rather than aspirational:
*"the text says X" ≠ "X is true"* — level is a FIELD, never inferred
from the presence of a text; and *"many traditions say X" ≠
"universally so"* — agreement is a computed edge, not a promotion,
because one source may carry claims at several levels (the
Foundation's principle, preserved as schema).

## 7 — First prototype (v0.1, the one sentence the friend wrote)
One session, one shelf (~100 hand-schema'd claim-records), one model
call per mouth + the rule-pack + the audit. **Ask Ascended a serious
question; receive a sourced, epistemic-labelled, constitutionally
checked synthesis with a visible trace.** Acceptance = (i) the
founder's five real questions — First Seeker's evidence, not
precedent, per the two-seats rule; (ii) a stage-test harness that runs
the rule-pack over *deliberately illegal drafts* (planted violations —
the spine must catch what the mouth would never have produced); and
(iii) the golden set re-ran against the full spine, so the law's
product-conformance is measured exactly as its mouth-conformance was.

## 8 — What we are NOT building
The friend's wall, plus mine. No Soul Log; no agents; no community,
Gate or token; no consciousness claims; no memory beyond the session
frame; no Traveler's-Map *office* (a row is not an institution); no
R-ladder UI (the conjunction rides the rule-pack, practice-cards on
demand); no personality engineering; no UI beyond a trace that reads;
no multi-model deliberation (two mouths later, one pipe now); no
streaming-optimization. And one law to keep on the wall: *the first
prototype tests the engine, not the ecosystem.*
