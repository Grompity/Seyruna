## 4. P4 — corroboration is a COUNT, never a claim

`corroboration(packet)` answers five narrow questions: how many addresses
rode, how many pages were actually READ, how many addresses are the
PROVIDER'S OWN house (attribution — the A4-live shape: four
duckduckgo.com stub-pages are NOT four voices), how many DISTINCT ORIGIN
publishers stand behind READ bytes, and the conservative word: `none`
(one voice reads nothing corroborated), `candidate` (≥2 distinct origins
READ — domain difference proves nothing stronger), or `unknown` (an
address never read is bytes not in hand). T139 pins every lane, including
the syndication one (same house twice = ONE publisher). The limits, stated:
a publisher's many domains would count twice; independence is never
ASSERTED; and the live eval showed the honest shape of THIS provider —
its word rode `none` or `unknown` for all six questions and never once
claimed more.

## 5. P5 — extraction, one failure seen live, one law moved

The A4-live witness named the concrete failure: the wikipedia claim rode
*"…Jump to content… From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia…"* where the
lead paragraph waited — chrome where content stood. The answer is three
laws, not a subsystem: BLOCK ENDS (paragraph, list, heading, blockquote,
detail, div, section, br) hold the lines apart; a LINE thinner than 60
bytes at a PAGE reads as furniture (title, jump-link, tagline — gone),
while a snippet passes `thin=0` because the index's short true words are
no chrome; and a CUT excerpt rides its own mark (`…`) — truncation that
speaks is not a claim of completeness. `<head>` joined the furniture list
(a title belongs to the SEARCH's title-field, not to the text).

The law's limit is stated, not hidden: a SHORT TRUE sentence under the bar
falls to it at a page (witnessed so it cannot be mistaken for wisdom).
One pre-existing fixture had to grow past the bar to stay a PAGE — the
fixture changed to the law's truth, not the law to the fixture.
