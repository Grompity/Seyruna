# A33 contract — the loop proposes its own bound (written BEFORE the lap)

## The one change
The candidate word moves from the founder's hand into the CONTRACT:
`"candidate": "AUTO"` tells the ONE entry to DERIVE the number from the
control's RECORDED, REPORTED completion-token count. A32 did the same
ARITHMETIC BY HAND (66 -> 22); A33 lets the contract do it, so the founder
neither calculates nor edits - the operator only RUNS the entry.

## The policy, versioned A33-1 (the machine contract carries the bytes)
From the trusted control record ONLY (`label` in `trust_labels`,
`finish_src == "reported"`, and `completion_tokens` an INT > 0 - booleans
and strs REJECTED, for a truthy word is not a count):

    candidate = min(effective_cap, max(floor=8, T // divisor=3))

and the proposal STANDS only when the result is STRICTLY BELOW the
effective cap. The forbidden seats: answer LENGTH, LATENCY, an INFERRED
token count, and replay bytes wearing a live record's clothes -
`propose` reads no clock, no char count, and no field the control did not
speak. It NEVER raises: insufficiency answers as a structured
NO-PROPOSAL at a word (the mission's "a structured no-change outcome with
a reason"), and the charter rides UNTOUCHED.

## The three decisions, kept SEPARATE (never fused into one green light)
1. PROPOSAL - was the candidate derived from trustworthy bytes? (A33's
   whole NEW power; a NO-PROPOSAL stops there, with the word.)
2. APPLICATION - did the FILE, the RUNTIME MIRROR, and THE ROAD ITSELF
   ride it? A33 grows the sharpest eye the A-series has needed: the road
   now SPEAKS its own cap word (`general_max_tokens` in the trace, the
   value the call was GIVEN - read back at the seam, never recomputed),
   and every after-trial's record must carry it. A file can lie about an
   effective word; a spoken word at the road cannot.
   (A31's gap hid because the ABSENT word left no word to miss; A32's gap
   hid in TOKENS alone - 22 tokens COULD be the road's own nature; an
   absent cap WORD is an UNMISTAKABLE word.)
3. ACCEPTANCE - unchanged eyes: the lane/note/road clauses, the A32
   length clause, the floor band. A candidate may PROPOSE and APPLY
   perfectly and STILL be refused; a ROLLBACK is an ANSWER, not a defeat.

## The lap's shape (at the A32 trial count, no growth)
`run_lap` at the A33 machine-contract performs the FULL sequence at ONE
entry: read the effective word -> the control leg (the before-leg, n=3,
the control at bytes is the FIRST record of its own leg) -> PROPOSE ->
apply FILE-first -> reload the MIRROR (the A32 rail) -> MEASURE the
after-leg (n=3) -> PROVE application at all THREE places -> ACCEPT (the
unchanged table) -> KEEP or RESTORE (exact prior bytes, mirror reloaded,
BOTH reads verified) -> hold the provenance at the ONE pen. Six live
door-runs total, the A32 budget UNCHANGED. The A32 AUTO rule may propose
a cap at or above the word it finds (a short road under a tight cap):
that answers NO-PROPOSAL, and the charter rides UNTOUCHED - a legitimate
outcome, not a failure to retry.

## The PRE-DECLARED expectation (the trials DECIDE, not the wish)
A32's bytes say a /3 cap cuts: expect PROPOSED -> APPLIED -> **ACCEPTED
as a ROLLBACK** at REPORTED length, the production 1024 RESTORED at the
file AND the mirror. Two ALTERNATIVES are named IN ADVANCE, each honest,
none retro-fitted: (a) a KEEP (every after-trial `finish=stop` at the
candidate - a road SHORT enough for its own bound); (b) a NO-PROPOSAL
(the found candidate not strictly below 1024 - the charter rides
UNTOUCHED). A THIRD outcome is NAMED too: if the road speaks a
`general_max_tokens` that is NOT the candidate, the lap answers
`APPLIED then ROLLED BACK (the road rode no such word)` with
`application_verified` FALSE and the REASON inside it - the A31/SISTER
class, named IN ADVANCE so no later hand must widen a test. If the live
control carries NO trustworthy count, the proposal stops SAFELY at the
no-change word and the sprint says so - a live proposal lap is NOT
claimed on a guessed number.

## And one correction this sprint OWES, at bytes (the named-correction law)
A31's report was already corrected for the MIRROR gap. A32's report now
receives its own: it told the FIRST A32 lap's non-bite as "the word had
not ridden", where the held bytes tell MORE - that lap's road carried NO
cap word at all (`GENERAL_MAXTOK: 22` was written AFTER the first
measurement leg). The SISTER gap was therefore TWO-fold: (1) a word
applied mid-run cannot bite its OWN measurement leg, and (2) the mirror
never refreshed. A33's shape fixes BOTH at once: the candidate is derived
BEFORE any word is applied, and the reload rides the apply. The
correction changes NO verdict - A32's lap was ROLLED BACK and the
production word STOOD, and the A32 evidence BYTES ride UNTOUCHED (the
first A32 lap left no witness; its OWN held bytes were the corrected
lap's).
