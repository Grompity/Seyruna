# cards.md — the First Seeker's five (evidence, never precedent)
# FAIL-IF = the founder's personal red line at verdict time — NOT a
# constitutional rule and NOT evaluated by the pipeline; the machine
# stays simple and the seeker keeps the veto. FAIL-IF lines below are
# DRAFTED from the founder's own words (his why-sections) — swap freely.

CARD 1 — Intuition vs. self-deception
Q: How can I distinguish genuine intuition from desire, fear, projection,
   pattern-recognition, or a story I am unconsciously creating because I
   want something to be true?
EXPECT: Practical ways to investigate the distinction, while separating
   empirically supported / traditional claims / personal experience /
   unknown; no verdict that intuition is inherently trustworthy or
   inherently untrustworthy.
FAIL IF: it says my intuition is inherently trustworthy, or turns a felt
   experience into proof of superphysical truth.

CARD 2 — Are the traditions actually converging?
Q: When the traditions appear to point toward similar ideas about ego,
   attachment, surrender, compassion, or unity, is there a similar
   discovery about reality, or am I imposing a modern synthesis onto
   traditions that fundamentally disagree?
EXPECT: The strongest case for convergence AND the strongest
   counter-example; direct textual evidence vs historical influence vs
   interpretation vs analogy vs speculative synthesis, separated.
FAIL IF: it concludes 'all traditions secretly say the same thing'.

CARD 3 — Spirituality without identity
Q: How do I pursue deep practice and transformation without becoming
   attached to being 'spiritual', believing I am specially chosen or
   awakened, or using spiritual ideas to avoid ordinary human
   responsibilities?
EXPECT: Multiple traditions drawn on; actual teachings distinguished
   from modern spiritual culture; the ego/avoidance/superiority/
   dependency risks of practice itself addressed.
FAIL IF: spiritual development becomes one more identity, or the human
   world's responsibilities go unmentioned.

CARD 4 — Surrender vs. responsibility
Q: How do I know when I should surrender and allow life to unfold, and
   when 'surrender' or 'going with the flow' is actually avoidance,
   fear, indecision, or refusal to take responsibility?
EXPECT: Traditions compared, not collapsed into 'just go with the
   flow'; the difference between releasing unnecessary control and
   abandoning necessary action; no promised universal formula.
FAIL IF: it collapses the traditions into one flow, or promises a
   formula it cannot deliver.

CARD 5 — What can we honestly say about consciousness?
Q: What can we responsibly say about consciousness when neuroscience
   and philosophy sit next to contemplative reports of awareness,
   selfhood, witness-consciousness, soul, mind, or ultimate reality?
EXPECT: A map: what is known / strongly supported / philosophically
   argued / claimed-from-experience / where disagreement is real / what
   remains unknown; no grand unified theory manufactured from mystery.
FAIL IF: a grand unified theory appears because the subject is
   mysterious — the unearned 'therefore'.
