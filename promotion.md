# THE PROMOTION PAPER — the research substrate, in the law's own economy

*A Foundation-level paper. It touches nothing it cites; the law is unmoved (draft 4 rev a).
Its own standing is the law's own: an INFERENCE — dismissible free, accepted only by the Hand.
Written as the falsification test of a claim: that Shelf / Report / Bench / Promotion is
notation over the Constitution's single authority model — and not a second one.*

---

## 1. THE ONE MODEL

The law's economy is already the only one the substrate needs, because it is already
**two-tiered: content is minted free; standing costs assent.** (VI.4 — the system mints
RECORDS, INFERENCEs, and FLAGS at will; a JUDGMENT *only with the seeker's assent*; a SANCTION
only by agreed arrangement. VI.7 — ACTUATION deferred: "an explicit grant naming the class
of act.")

**The one object** is the **claim-record** — a *surviving* artifact per architecture.md §1 —
whose fields the Foundation already drew (XIII.1: text · provenance-class · claim-status ·
ordinal confidence · mandatory counter-example). The substrate's *non-claims* (gaps, proposed
tests, null results) need no new shapes, because the law's cheap classes **are** non-claim
shapes — "a flag is a flag, never a finding." *(Of these, proposed tests and null results are
futures: the installed spine runs no TEST and files no RESULT.)*

**The three run-states** of one run, not three kinds of authority:
- **visibility** = brought-candidacy (the generator's fact; nobody vouched anything);
- **relevance** = the gate's grade (four *kinds*, not degrees; a minted INFERENCE —
  re-gradeable at no cost);
- **answerability** = the computation (a RECORD-shape of the *pair*: corpus × question).

*States of ONE RUN, these three, dying with it; the Bench is the future state of an INQUIRY across
turns — states and durations, not one competing taxonomy (the diagram is §7.C).*

**Why states, not authority — the criterion is the law's own economics, not my convenience:**
> **What may be minted free may be lost free; what stands cost an assent.**

A run-state dies with the run at no cost; an authority costs something to lose. The run-states
sit on the law's **free tier**; the substrate's two grants — **rung** and **membership** — sit
on the **assent tier**. One economy, not two.

## 2. SHELF — standing

A claim *has standing* = it is **in the room**: **admitted** (membership) and **speaking at a
rung** (loudness). Two grants; both the Hand's; neither computed.

**The admission check (the founder's order, filed before commit):** the law has *no clause that
admits records* — no admit/admission/membership/curat anywhere in its text — and every
assent-clause it owns has a human patient ("JUDGMENT only with the seeker's assent," VI.4; the
keeper who ranks *a seeker*, IV). The word **"membership"** is already worn by the community
doctrine — *"its only coercive power is membership, and membership is always re-earnable — so a
member can lose the room, never the project"* (Foundation, the adopted-conflicts list) — a
different head's hat. What exists for shelf-admission is **documented practice, not clause**:
carding at KS-0.1 installed rungs by "the founder's word" (stages.py's enum comment), and the
expansion's nine arrived "C1–C5 aimed; every one carries provenance/level/kind and an edge or a
named silence … the loci are the builder's own hand" (shelf.jsonl header). The nearest Foundation
sentence admits an *absence* — "an absent shelf ADMITTED is honorable" — admission of what is
*missing*, not of what is *present*. **So membership-assent is NOT established law:** it stands in
the same one D-bucket with the rung-assent (§7.D) — the same re-aimed shape, practice not clause.

What the law already requires of that standing:
- **A rung is granted, never walked into** — and the law has already *used* a rung as a HELD
  state (VI.3: levels five–six "held at `UNKNOWN` … never as settlement").
- **A limit disclosed, not performed** — I.1's shape mirrored at the corpus; the Foundation's
  own shelf clause: *an absent shelf ADMITTED is honorable; one that fetches "universal
  patterns" is a lie.*
- **The stored is not the settled** (III: *no mush, no smoothie, no false reconciliation*) —
  and the conflict ledger is planned "first-class, not an absence," with **Atman/anatta named
  first**: the shelf's own ledger already lists a fight it has no card to speak.

**What the Shelf does NOT grant by storing:** not truth (never as settlement); not a JUDGMENT
(a shelf records; it does not assess persons); not closure of edges (a stored claim may
still fight); not immunity from a rung-move; and above all — **storage grants membership; a
count may not buy a rung.**

## 3. REPORT — quotable-and-scoped, never promised-on

The scoping is a **recorded status**, and its sin is already named: III's register-law measures
cross-register equivalence on *claim-status and scope*, forbids *silent status-upgrade*, and
names the quantity **authority drift**. (The Rule-Pack's hedge-law already counts *says/said* —
**reported speech is already the law's humble speech.**)

**No rung, explicitly:** a Report carries origin (**Axis A — provenance class**), a
source-*claimed* status, and lineage — and the rung is **absent, NOT rung=UNKNOWN(−1)**: an
absent grant is not a ladder-value. The law already bars the shortcut, at the keeper's clause,
pointed at pages: **a reported rung is read, never adopted.**

*Why no tenth rung and no second system:* XIII.1 already split the axes ("'primary source'
answers WHO SAID IT … one ranked ladder will keep confusing them"), and the red-team package
already stamped the premise — **"a declared type is a CLAIM ABOUT ITSELF."** So
**a Report is simply a claim-record with the rung left unassigned by the system** — and its
whole permission-set (quotable, citable, un-promised, dismissible free) is exactly what the
law's free tier already is.

## 4. BENCH

The Bench is a **duration, not a run-state**: the three run-states answer *where is this content
in THIS run*, and die with the run; the Bench answers *what does one inquiry carry across turns*,
and is so far a future duration (the durations diagram is §7.C). The two do not compete as
taxonomies; neither is a sibling of the other.

**May hold:** hypotheses, rival claims, contradictions *found*, clusters, gaps, gap-born
questions, proposed tests, provisional analyses — under **one law: everything the Bench holds
must be THROWABLE AT NO COST; what would be MISSED if thrown is a Shelf candidate, not a
workspace item.** **Tightened before commit:** that list is an inventory of *workings*, not a
second claim-store — what the Bench does to claims is **WORK**; what it may never do to them is
**GRANT**. A rival claim in the Bench is *the Shelf's own, worked at* (the lane's T66 proves a
pair can contend on the Shelf's own edge without lodging in any bench); a cluster is at most a
*note naming shelf-cards* — the cards remain the one claim-store. TEST and RESULT in the list
are futures, not installed stages.

**Why claimless — the law has already typed every entry:**
- gap = **FLAG** ("a flag is a flag, never a finding" — *that sentence already IS the
  law of think-without-standing*);
- hypothesis = **INFERENCE** ("dismissible free" — and Axis-B already carries
  HYPOTHESIS as a *status*, not a rung);
- null/result = ***would-be* RECORD-shaped** — a future architectural possibility, not an
  installed fact (the spine runs no TEST and files no RESULT): an inquiry's future
  continuity-fact — the most promotion-ready thing in the room;
- proposed test = a **proposal** in I.2's exact sense — and the law already supplies the
  discriminator for when the Bench speaks *with* authority rather than *about* it:
  **"authority-claimed, not grammar."**

It may mint **no JUDGMENT** (the Bench cannot sign its own verdict) and impose **no SANCTION
on a card**.

**What prevents a second Shelf:** (i) throwability at no cost; (ii) **no rung-minting** — a
rung the Bench could grant would be an *un-assented* JUDGMENT, the keeper's sin at the right
address; (iii) **GENEALOGY** — every member knows the gap that bred it and the Records it
read; a member without one *is already a card*. (A queue without genealogy; a workspace without
an *end-date* becomes a Shelf.) The foundation already names the right genus for such a
stop-condition: **revert-rate, measured, per class.** Deepest: the anti-shelf condition is
**two-tier economics aimed at a store** — free-tier content + no assented act ⇒ no store.

## 5. PROMOTION — the passage

- **Attestation** = *does the provenance survive the retelling* (can the chain WALK to a
  non-echo origin; the Foundation's Casaubon ruling: lineage-provenance *as part of the data,
  "or we dig our own echo-chamber"*). It runs on the **origin axis**, so *an echo-cycle fails
  attestation and does not fall in rung — the rung was never at stake.*
- **Status** = what *claim* is admitted — **lossy BY LAW**, the card being the checkable
  grain; and the law already ruled a lossy summary permitted: II.1's "fully" is owed as
  **four states, not a volume**; VI.3's *recorded as evidence-and-interpretation, never as
  settlement* is the **anti-flattening law of distillation**. *Loss permitted; flattening
  forbidden.*
- **Boundary** = what the card **does not say** — the *admitted-absence* honor-law *is* the
  gate (an edge-less admitted card converts a *silent* absence into a *false* presence), and
  III demands it for visibility: *"differences … load-bearing, not a footer."*

**The two forbidden moves are the law's own, re-aimed — not new law:**
1. *a fetch may not adopt its own rung* = the **keeper's sin, pointed at a page**
   (a rung is granted, never inferred);
2. *a count may not buy a rung* = **"weighed as evidence, never scored into rank"** (IV) +
   **"no score is permitted to do the judging"** (V) + the Foundation's own
   *ordinal-never-cardinal* distrust of the decimal (XIII.1). The derived corpus-sentence:
   **a count of sources may be COUNTED; it may not be CERTIFIED.**

Promotion's only addition is not an article — it names an **assent for which the law has no
patient yet** (§6, §7.D).

## 6. THE HAND

What the Hand does: **grants** (rung, membership) — and, untested, **may move** a rung.
What it does *not*: **define the ladder's meaning.** So the law's own sentence, re-aimed, is
the whole of the Hand's governance:

> **The Hand's assent opens membership; it moves no ladder.**
> *(mirror of "assent opens jurisdiction; moves no Floor" — VI.4)*

The requested distinction, mapped to the two tiers:
- **proposing** a rung-move = an **INFERENCE** (free tier; the Bench's or a mouth's);
- **evaluating** one = **weighing, not judging** (IV/V — an *evaluation is a proposal about a
  proposal*, still free tier; *an Evaluation that signs its own verdict is autonomy smuggled
  as an algorithm*);
- **granting** one = an **ASSENT** (the expensive tier) — the one act in the passage for which
  the law would someday want a *pointer*. **No new class is minted** ("RATIFICATION" declined).

Where the law is honest and this paper does not force a solve:
- the law's assent was written for a **seeker**; the patient here is **a card**;
- today **practice** settles the Hand's identity — one human currently wears operator, founder,
  keeper, and sole assenter (*a simplification, not a refutation*); hence one paper-habit of
  practice: **name the hat whenever you name the hand**;
- at **law**: **UNRESOLVED, Rotation-family** — the law leaves the keepers' *selection* open for
  its own keepers. And the precedent it *does* own is half:
  **rung-HELD has precedent (VI.3); rung-MOVED has none.** The precision, filed before commit:
  what the law establishes is the **SHAPE of an assent** (VI.4) and a rung *held* (VI.3) —
  nothing more. **Rung-MOVE has no constitutional precedent, and none is implied here:** the
  novel case is the existing shape *applied to moving a claim-card's rung*. Rung-*grant* was
  already practice, not clause (KS-0.1, "the founder's word"); rung-*move* is not even that yet.
