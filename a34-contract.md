# A34 contract — a fair duel of bounds, then the climb (written before outcomes)

## Part 1: the comparison, fixed BEFORE the bytes
**Policy A** re-endorses the standing law (`A33-1`: candidate =
min(effective, max(8, T//3))). **Policy B** is the half-bound
(`A34-B1`: candidate = min(effective, max(8, T//2))) — the SAME floor
(8), the SAME strict-below law, the SAME trust gates (label, reported
src, INT > 0), the SAME eyes (the A32 criteria, the A33 road-word
requirement). The two policies differ at exactly ONE number - the
divisor - so the comparison is honest: no formula quietly gained a
shield the other lacks. The half-bound (T//2) is the LOOSER cap: it
cuts less often and bounds the tail less tightly. The trade the sprint
buys with one number: a looser cap more likely to leave the road WHOLE;
a tighter cap that bounds the runaway harder.

**Fairness (the mission's law, honored):** same question, same model,
same endpoint, same prompt, same road, same validation, same trial
budget (the A33 law: n=3 per side, six door-runs per leg), the same
per-lap control (each policy's own before-leg is its control - the
proposal is the loop's, never the operator's). Order is FIXED and
DISCLOSED (A first, then B) - the endpoint drifts (A29's band) and a
cross-over pair is reserved: if the decision rests on anything tight
(or one leg answers the unexpected class), ONE confirming pair (B then
A) is spent - and only ONE, no chasing. Per-trial records ride the
held bytes (aggregates are summaries, never the evidence).

## The acceptance and the decision table (pre-declared, deterministic)
Per leg, the loop's OWN artifacts classify the support-word:
`KEPT` (the verdict rode), `ROLLED-CUT` (rolled AT a reported length),
`ROLLED-UNCUT` (rolled at a non-length word - a correctness failure),
`NO-PROPOSAL`, `APP-FAIL` (the word did not ride all three places).
Verdicts, exhaustive over the pairs:
- exactly one KEPT and the other ROLLED-CUT -> **the KEPT side is
  better supported** (A kept: A33-1 stands by evidence; B kept: the
  half-bound is better supported - the ONLY route to a B-win);
- BOTH KEPT -> A33-1 SAFER-SUPPORTED (both complete; the TIGHTER bound
  is the safer bound, now PROVEN adequate);
- BOTH ROLLED-CUT -> A33-1 SAFER-SUPPORTED (equal correctness; the
  tighter bound bounds the tail harder; and NO cap was found liveable,
  so the safe PRODUCTION word 1024 rides UNTOUCHED - the mission's own
  retain-the-safe-config outcome, NOT a failure to chase);
- any NO-PROPOSAL, ROLLED-UNCUT, or APP-FAIL -> **NEITHER-JUSTIFIED**,
  with the words (insufficient evidence or unclear correctness - the
  sprint reports it, does not chase it).
NO speed claim: latencies inside the disclosed band (A29's ~30%) carry
NO decision weight (the A33-AFTER medians already sit under the band,
and a STALL (56,948ms, A33's bytes) proves a single trial decides
nothing). A faster TRUNCATED output LOSES to correctness, never wins
on speed.

## PART 2 addendum — the cycle's law, declared before its bytes

A22-era record with the packet's OWN query word and the door's OWN
status words; two stages = two rides). New words, no new machinery:

OWN fields, never from prose):**
1. `RETRIEVAL-FAIL` (the lane's own word - ok false or gaps non-empty);
2. `SEARCH-EMPTY` (ok at zero results);
3. `NO-ORIGIN` (the A7 word - provider words only, no origin read);
4. `SINGLE-ORIGIN` (fewer than two origins with a READ text);
5. `SUFFICIENT` (no gap to close; the follow-up then tests BREADTH, and
   the sprint says so rather than faking a gap).
**The follow-up queries (A34-C1, deterministic words over record words):**
RETRIEVAL-FAIL -> question + " overview" (the retry idiom, named as such);

SUFFICIENT -> question + " in summary" (breadth, declared). The query
rides the A22 FROZEN-QUERY rail (the question's bytes ride the door

NO-MATERIAL-IMPROVEMENT - a VALID FINDING, printed as one.
